Privacy Policy

Creative Math Zone · class management portal

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

Creative Math Zone is a coaching centre. This website and its apps (the admin, teacher and student portals) are used by the centre, its teachers, its students and their parents to manage classes, attendance, homework, doubts and fees.

What information we keep

  • Student details given at registration: name, class, board, school, date of birth, contact numbers, email, address and parent/guardian details.
  • Teacher details: name, contact details and the subjects they teach.
  • Learning records: subjects, batches, timetable, attendance, homework and submissions, and doubts with their answers and photos.
  • Fee records: invoices, payments, receipts and discounts. We do not store card or bank passwords; UPI payments are made in the payer’s own app.
  • Account and security data: username, sign-in times and an activity log of changes made by staff.

How we use it

Only to run the centre: teaching, attendance, homework and doubt solving, sending notices, preparing invoices and receipts, and paying teachers. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

  • Records are stored in a secure database hosted by Supabase. Only signed-in users can reach their own information, and staff access depends on their role.
  • Homework files, doubt photos and weekly backups are stored in the centre’s own Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The portal connects to the centre’s Google account with the limited “files created by this app” permission (drive.file). It can only create, read and delete the files it saved itself — homework files, doubt photos and backups. It cannot see any other files in that Google Drive. Data received from Google is used only to store and show these files inside the portal and is not shared with anyone else.

Who can see what

  • Students see their own records, and doubts posted by classmates in the same class and subject.
  • Teachers see the students and doubts of the subjects they teach.
  • Centre administrators see the records needed to run the centre.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while a student studies at the centre and for as long as needed for fee and accounting records. Class PDFs are removed after seven days. A student’s records can be permanently deleted by the centre on request.

Your choices

Students and parents can ask the centre office to see, correct or delete their information. Students can update some contact details themselves from the Profile page of the student portal.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. Their information is provided by them or their parents/guardians at admission and is used only for their studies at the centre.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, please contact the Creative Math Zone office.
